## Fix round 1 (applied after the `fix` disposition; verdict pass requested on these six)

1. **Traffic as light** (`NightshiftHero.tsx`, `Traffic`): the sphere halo and the opaque streak/tail boxes are gone. Each vehicle is now three additive, depth-write-off ShaderMaterial instances: a trail of two crossed quads (4.6 long, 0.26 wide) whose alpha peaks at the head and falls down the tail (pow 1.8) with soft edges across; a camera-facing halo billboard (1.15, Blue Spot, radial falloff pow 2.4, opacity 0.8) with no silhouette; and a core billboard (0.36, Packet Core, pow 1.6). Trails are oriented by heading (tangent times direction) so the head is always the bright end. The work light's cone is softer (angle 0.55, penumbra 0.9, intensity 700). Sodium posts went from 240 to 380 so the deck shows its pools, and Tarmac lifted to #30363d. Evidence: `nightshift-desktop-interchange.png` (streaks at ~850,715 with a soft glow, no discs), `nightshift-desktop-ramps.png` (trails along the deck), `nightshift-desktop-overpass.png`.
2. **Exit gated on the scene** (`SiteIntro.astro` script + CSS, `NightshiftHero.tsx`, `scene.ts`): the Night Shift block declares `data-intro-gate="ns-out" data-intro-exit-at="1100" data-intro-exit-at-quick="180"`. The curtain script arms a gate at play (arrival) or at start (quick): the tunnel's exit (`ns-out`, `animation-play-state: paused` by default) runs only once `#site-intro` carries `data-exit`, which the gate sets when both the minimum time has passed and `<html data-scene="drawn">` exists, with a 1.5s grace after the minimum so a scene that never reports still exits; `finish()` now fires on `ns-out`'s end for gated worlds (intro-root pushed out to 6s / 4s as a ceiling; safety timers +1.5s). The hero sets `data-scene="drawn"` from `FirstFrames` (or at once when there is no WebGL) and clears it whenever the world goes inactive, so a later quick-change waits again. Evidence: `intro-nightshift-exit-400.png` (the tunnel 400ms into its exit over a hero whose scene is already drawn: deck, sign and lamp visible through it), `intro-nightshift-after.png`, `qc-nightshift-done-reveal.png`. Instrumented in-page timelines (MutationObserver on data-intro / data-scene / data-exit, animationend on ns-out) confirmed the sequence: the exit attribute appears only after the minimum, `ns-out` ends exactly at `done`, and the hero writes `data-scene` (verified after arrival). On this software renderer the scene takes several seconds to compile, so the grace path is what these captures exercise; the drawn path was exercised by simulating the scene's report at play (`intro-nightshift-exit-400.png` comes from that run: gate opened while the intro was still `reveal`).
3. **Tunnel that encloses and converges** (`SiteIntro.astro`): the two strips (lamps overhead, lane underfoot) now share one perspective (300px at 33.6deg, horizon at half the viewport), are 600% tall and masked out just before the vanishing point; two wall wedges (`clip-path` from each side edge to the centre, lit at the near edge, dark toward the centre) enclose the space; the rushing patterns moved to `::before` layers animated with transforms. The first cut of this (1300% strips plus 1000% 3D walls) blocked the main thread for seconds under software raster, which is what made the earlier timer-based captures show the curtain gone at 650ms; the slim version cut the pre-reveal long tasks to none. Evidence: `intro-nightshift-250.png`, `-650.png`, `-1000.png`, `-1350.png` (captured by freezing the curtain's animations in-page with the Web Animations API and seeking them to exact times, so they are exact regardless of the renderer), `qc-nightshift-150-cover.png`.
